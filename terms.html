SEO Keyword Collector — Terms of Use

How this independent local SEO utility is intended to be used.

Effective date: 13 October 2026

1. Purpose of the tool

SEO Keyword Collector is a private desktop utility for aggregating and organizing historical search metrics from Google Ads Keyword Planner. Its main workflow is importing keyword lists, selecting Google Ads locations and languages, requesting historical metrics, and exporting local SEO reports. It is currently being configured and requires an authorized Google Ads account to operate.

2. Access and permissions

Users must have legal permission to use each Google Ads account connected to the utility and must authorize access with Google OAuth. Use is subject to Google Ads API rules, quotas, Google APIs Terms of Service, and applicable law. The tool is not intended to alter campaigns or submit advertising spend.

3. What the tool does not do

This application does not generate or edit images, videos or other media and has no generative AI functions. It does not use Google APIs for non-consensual intimate imagery. It is not an advertising network, and does not sell Google account information or search statistics. It is independent of Google LLC.

4. Estimates and service availability

Historical keyword metrics are Google's estimates, not exact counts. Some similar keywords may be combined. Coverage varies by country, language and account; Google Ads API access may require an approved developer access level. Features are supplied as available, without any guarantee of precision or continuous access.

5. Data and deletion

Data handling is described in our Privacy Policy. Results and access credentials are intended to remain on the authorized user's local device. Users can delete local files and revoke OAuth consent in their Google Account.

6. Project contact

The operator's support email is listed in the Google OAuth consent screen. This informational website is publicly accessible without login and does not provide a hosted account or data collection portal.

Условия использования — на русском

SEO Keyword Collector — частная локальная программа для получения оценок частотности из Google Ads API (Keyword Planner). Пользователь импортирует список запросов, выбирает язык и регион, выполняет авторизацию Google и сохраняет результаты в CSV и локальную базу.

Для работы необходим законный доступ к Google Ads и соблюдение условий API. Программа не размещает рекламу, не генерирует изображения, не изменяет рекламные кампании и не продаёт данные пользователя. Статистические данные могут быть неполными, агрегированными и приблизительными. Программа пока находится в стадии настройки и предоставляется без гарантий доступности Google API.

Работа с пользовательскими данными подробно описана в политике конфиденциальности.
